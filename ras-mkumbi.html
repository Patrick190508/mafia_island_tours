Full Day Tour

Ras Mkumbi Lighthouse
+ Banja Beach Lunch
+ Hippo Tour

The best of everything Mafia Island has to offer — all in one unforgettable day.

Overview

This is our signature full-day experience. We start early in the morning and head north across the island towards Ras Mkumbi — the northernmost tip of Mafia, where a historic lighthouse stands overlooking the open Indian Ocean. Then we drive south to Banja Beach for a relaxed lunch on the sand. As the afternoon winds down, we head to the hippo lagoon for a guided observation before returning to your hotel.

Expect to see mangrove forests, local fishing villages, untouched coastline and wildlife that most visitors to Tanzania never get to see.

Itinerary

1

Morning pickup — 7:30 AM

We pick you up from your hotel or guesthouse in our 4×4. Coffee and water provided for the road.

2

Drive north — through the island

We cross Mafia from south to north, passing through villages, forest tracks and coastal viewpoints. Short stops for photos.

3

Ras Mkumbi Lighthouse

Arrive at the northernmost tip of the island. The lighthouse offers panoramic views of the Indian Ocean. We usually have the place entirely to ourselves.

4

Banja Beach — Lunch

Drive down to Banja Beach — one of the most pristine beaches on the island. Freshly prepared local lunch on the sand. Swim, relax, snorkel.

5

Hippo Tour — late afternoon

Head to the hippo lagoon as the light softens. Observe hippos in their natural habitat from a safe distance. One of the most unexpected highlights of Mafia Island.

6

Return to your hotel — ~6:00 PM

We drive you back as the sun sets over the island.

What's included

  • Private 4×4 vehicle and driver/guide all day
  • Freshly prepared lunch at Banja Beach
  • Water and light snacks during the drive
  • Hippo lagoon visit with guide
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off

What to bring

  • Sunscreen and hat — it gets very sunny
  • Swimwear and towel
  • Comfortable shoes for walking on uneven terrain
  • Camera / phone for photos
  • Small cash if you'd like to buy anything from local vendors
