Taxi Service

Taxi Service

Need to get somewhere on Mafia Island? Send us a message and we'll be there.

The service

Our taxi service covers the whole island — from Kilindoni town to the remote southern tip. We're available most hours of the day and respond quickly on WhatsApp.

This is the simplest way to get around Mafia Island without renting a car or relying on unreliable local options. Just message us your location, destination and when you need to leave.

Typical rides

  • Kilindoni town ↔ Utende / Chole Bay
  • Hotel ↔ restaurant or bar
  • Any location ↔ local market or shop
  • Quick rides within the same area
  • Evening pickup after dinner
  • Any other on-island destination

Our vehicle

We use a Suzuki Escudo 4×4 — handles every road on the island comfortably, with space for up to 4 passengers and luggage.

How it works

WhatsApp us your pickup point and destination. We'll confirm if we're available and give you a price before you commit. No surprises, no haggling on arrival.

Notes

  • Up to 4 passengers per ride
  • Luggage space available
  • English-speaking driver
  • Advance booking preferred but same-day usually possible
