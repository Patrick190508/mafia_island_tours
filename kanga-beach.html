Half Day Tour

Kanga Beach Relax & Lunch
+ Hippo Tour

A slow afternoon on the most beautiful beach on the island — followed by the wild surprise of hippos at dusk.

Overview

Kanga Beach is one of Mafia Island's best-kept secrets — white sand, turquoise water and almost no one there. We bring you for a relaxed afternoon: swim, snorkel, read a book, or just watch the horizon. A freshly prepared local lunch is served on the beach.

As the afternoon light turns golden, we drive to the hippo lagoon. Watching hippos in the wild on a small island in Tanzania is an experience that surprises every visitor.

Itinerary

1

Pickup — 10:30 AM

We collect you from your accommodation and head straight to Kanga.

2

Kanga Beach — morning/early afternoon

Arrive at the beach. Swim, snorkel or simply relax. There's nothing to do here except enjoy it.

3

Lunch on the beach

Freshly prepared local food served in the shade. Fish, rice, coconut — Swahili coastal cuisine at its best.

4

Hippo Tour — late afternoon

Drive to the hippo lagoon. Guided observation from a safe distance as hippos surface and move through the water.

5

Return — ~5:30 PM

We drop you back at your hotel in time for sunset.

What's included

  • Private 4×4 vehicle and guide
  • Freshly prepared lunch on the beach
  • Water during the trip
  • Hippo lagoon guided visit
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off

What to bring

  • Swimwear and towel
  • Sunscreen and hat
  • Snorkel gear (optional — we can advise on rental)
  • Camera
