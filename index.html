Where we are

Mafia Island, Tanzania

A small island in the Indian Ocean, 130 km south of Dar es Salaam. Part of a UNESCO-recognised marine park. Home to whale sharks, hippos, ancient ruins and some of the least-crowded beaches in East Africa.

We're based in Utende, near the marine park entrance — the heart of the island's best experiences.
