Transfer Service

Island Car Transfer

Private 4×4 transfers to any point on Mafia Island. Airport, hotel, port, village — anytime you need.

The service

Mafia Island's roads range from paved to rough track. Our 4×4 handles all of them comfortably, and we know every route on the island. Whether you've just landed at the airport, need to reach a remote dive site, or want to visit a village on the other side of the island, we get you there reliably and on time.

This is a point-to-point private transfer — no shared vehicles, no waiting for other passengers, no uncertainty about whether the car will show up.

Common routes

  • Mafia Airport (MBA) ↔ Utende / Chole Bay area
  • Airport ↔ any hotel or guesthouse on the island
  • Kilindoni port ↔ anywhere on the island
  • Hotel ↔ dive centre / snorkelling site
  • Cross-island routes (north ↔ south)
  • Custom: any pickup and drop-off you need

How to book

Send us a WhatsApp message with your pickup location, destination, date and time. We'll confirm availability and price immediately. For airport pickups, share your flight details so we can track delays.

Vehicle

We operate a Suzuki Escudo 4×4 — reliable on Mafia Island's mix of paved road and rough track, suitable for luggage, dive equipment and up to 4 passengers.
